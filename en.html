Clear information is the starting point for a more precise quotation.

What information should a quotation request include?

Include the product name, brand, model or part number, quantity and key specifications. You can also add your budget and preferred delivery timeframe.

How is international equipment sourcing arranged?

We assess international sourcing options based on your requested model and specifications. Availability, pricing, estimated lead times and delivery terms are agreed for each enquiry.

Can I send an existing procurement list?

Yes. Send your equipment list via WhatsApp or as an email attachment to info@nordatech.az. Including part numbers and quantities helps us clarify your enquiry.
